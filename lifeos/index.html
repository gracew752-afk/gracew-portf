the rules
No new pages, databases, or planners unless they're tied to a project that's Locked this quarter. Future Grace can build her dream dashboard after she ships.
Research caps at 3 hours a week and must end in at least one card. If it didn't produce a card, it was just scrolling with extra steps.
Jealousy is a brief. If something online makes me feral, it becomes a response piece in the Parking Lot within 24 hours. Envy is free creative direction.
Ship ugly before you polish pretty. A shipped 7/10 campaign beats a Notion page about a 10/10 one. Every time. It's not close.
One hero per quarter. The other genius ideas can sit in the Parking Lot and think about what they did.
The grid is a model book, not a diary. Every post earns its spot: portfolio proof, modeling proof, or product sales.
20+ minutes in Notion without touching a deliverable? Close the tab. The OS is a launchpad, not a hobby.
